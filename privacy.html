Masjid Aaisha — Privacy Policy

Masjid Aaisha app for iPhone and Apple Watch

Effective date: 26 November 2026
Provided by: Mohammed Abdul Rehman, for Masjid Aaisha, Paramount Hills, Tolichowki, Hyderabad, Telangana, India
Contact: abdurrahman.yamin@icloud.com

Summary

  • The app shows Masjid Aaisha’s prayer times, Iqāmah times and Hijri date.
  • It has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • It does not ask for or use your device’s location (GPS / Location Services), contacts, photos, camera, microphone or other personal content.
  • To receive the Masjid’s timetable, the iPhone app connects to our data provider. Like most internet services, the provider automatically records basic technical details of each connection, described below.

Information processed when the app connects

When the iPhone app downloads the timetable or listens for timetable updates, it connects over HTTPS to our data provider, Supabase. The app sends no name, account, contact details or other personal content. As with any internet connection, the request necessarily includes technical information. According to Supabase’s published documentation, its request logs can record:

  • your device’s IP address;
  • an approximate location derived from that IP address (for example, city or region), added by the network that carries the request. This is not your device’s GPS location;
  • technical details such as the app version, the device’s operating system version, and the time and address of the request.

Why: these logs exist so the service can be delivered, kept secure and kept running (app functionality). We do not use them for advertising, analytics, profiling or tracking. We do not sell or share them, and we do not combine them with other data.

How long: under the plan we use, Supabase’s documentation states that these request logs are retained for about one day. Supabase acts as our service provider (data processor) under its published data processing terms. Retention and processing by Supabase are governed by its own policies, which may change; see supabase.com/privacy.

Because an IP address can identify a person under data-protection laws, the app’s App Store privacy label conservatively lists this information as Coarse Location and Diagnostics, linked to you, used only for app functionality and not for tracking.

Information kept on your devices

The downloaded prayer timetable, the Hijri calendar and your in-app settings (for example, the Live Activity switch) are stored only on your iPhone and Apple Watch, so the app keeps working offline. The Apple Watch app, widgets, Watch complications and Live Activities receive their information from your iPhone; they do not connect to the internet themselves. This information is removed when you delete the app.

This website

These Support and Privacy Policy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. The pages themselves use no cookies, analytics, tracking or third-party scripts. As the hosting provider, GitHub may process technical information about visits to these pages (such as your IP address) under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This is separate from the app, and visiting these pages is not required to use it.

Children

The app contains no accounts, advertising or user-generated content and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the technical connection information described above.

Your choices

  • You can use the app offline: it keeps working with the timetable already stored on your device.
  • Deleting the app removes all information stored on your devices.
  • For questions or requests about this policy, contact abdurrahman.yamin@icloud.com.

Changes

We will update this policy if the app’s data practices change, and show the new effective date above.

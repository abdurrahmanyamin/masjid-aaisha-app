About the app

  • Shows today’s prayer and Iqāmah times as published by Masjid Aaisha, with a live countdown to the next Adhān or Iqāmah.
  • Shows the Masjid’s Hijri date, which changes at Maghrib.
  • Includes Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, an optional Live Activity, and an Apple Watch app with complications.
  • Has no accounts and no advertising.

Requirements

  • iPhone with iOS 26.5 or later.
  • Apple Watch with watchOS 26.5 or later (optional), paired with an iPhone that has the app installed.
  • An internet connection to receive timetable updates. The app also works offline with the timetable already stored on the device.

Frequently asked questions

How does the app receive the timetable?
The Masjid publishes its timetable and Hijri date. While the iPhone app is open and online, it receives changes within seconds. It also checks for updates when you open it.

The times or Hijri date look out of date.
Open the iPhone app while connected to the internet. It downloads the latest timetable and passes it to your Apple Watch.

My Apple Watch has not updated yet.
Open the app on your iPhone, then open it on your Watch. The Watch receives its information from your iPhone.

A widget or Watch complication still shows old information.
iOS and watchOS decide when widgets and complications refresh. Opening the app usually prompts an update.

Does the app work without internet?
Yes. It uses the timetable already stored on your device. New changes from the Masjid arrive the next time you are online.

Does the app use my location?
No. The times are for Masjid Aaisha only, and the app does not use Location Services.
